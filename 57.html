Determination of Relative Density of a Liquid

Physics Formula:
$h_w \cdot \rho_w \cdot g = h_l \cdot \rho_l \cdot g$
$h_w = \left(\frac{\rho_l}{\rho_w}\right) \cdot h_l = d \cdot h_l$
Gradient ($m$) = Relative Density ($d$)
