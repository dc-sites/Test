Vernier Caliper Simulator

Interactive tool for learning precision measurements and reading alignment.

Reading: 0.00 mm (0.000 cm)

How to Use the Simulator

  1. Drag Jaws & Slider: Click/touch and drag the movable jaw directly or use the Adjust slider for fine placement.
  2. Set Least Count: Use the Least Count dropdown to select scale precision ($0.1\text{ mm}$, $0.05\text{ mm}$, $0.02\text{ mm}$, or $0.5\text{ mm}$).
  3. Zoom Lens: Click Zoom Lens to reveal a dynamic magnifying glass that tracks Vernier scale alignments.
  4. Practice Mode: Click Hide Reading and Random Object to measure objects and test your scale reading skills.
