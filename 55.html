$P_0 + \rho_w \cdot g \cdot h_w = P_0 + \rho_l \cdot g \cdot h_l$

$\implies \text{Relative Density } (d) = \frac{\rho_l}{\rho_w} = \frac{h_w}{h_l} = \text{Gradient of Graph}$
