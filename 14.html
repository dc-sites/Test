Resonance Tube Experiment

Theory & Formulas

Measurement of the velocity of sound in air (v) and end correction (e) using a closed resonating tube.

• Fundamental Resonance:   ¼ λ = l1 + e  ⇒  v = 4f(l1 + e)
• First Overtone:   ¾ λ = l2 + e  ⇒  v = &frac43; f(l2 + e)
• Velocity of Sound:   v = 2f(l2 − l1)
• End Correction:   e = (l2 − 3l1) / 2
