Theory & Verification

Formula: $T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{m}{k}} \implies T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{k}\right) m$

Plotting $T^2$ against $m$ yields a straight line through the origin, verifying $T^2 \propto m$.
